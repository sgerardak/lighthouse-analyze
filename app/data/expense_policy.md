# Sample Expense & Finance Policy

> Fictional policy created for a technical exercise. It is not Lighthouse's actual policy.
> All amounts are in EUR.

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy explains how employees spend company money, how they document it, and what they need to do during the month-end close.

### 1.2 Who this applies to
This policy applies to all employees and contractors of every company entity who hold a company card, submit expenses, receive vendor invoices, or own a budget.

### 1.3 Where to get help
Questions not covered by this policy go to the Finance team in the #finance-help Slack channel. Finance aims to reply within one business day.

## 2. Company cards (Brex)

### 2.1 Who gets a card
Company cards are issued by Finance to employees who regularly spend on behalf of the company, with their manager's approval. Each card has a monthly limit set by Finance.

### 2.2 Allowed use
Company cards may only be used for business expenses that follow this policy, such as business travel, client meals, team events, and approved software or services.

### 2.3 Not allowed
The following are never allowed on a company card: personal expenses, cash withdrawals, traffic fines or penalties, expenses for family members or friends, and splitting one purchase into several transactions to stay under a limit.

### 2.4 Personal charges made by mistake
If a personal expense is charged to a company card by mistake, the employee must report it in the #finance-help channel within 5 business days. Finance will share the details to repay the amount by bank transfer.

## 3. Receipts and documentation

### 3.1 Receipt requirements
Every card transaction needs a receipt uploaded in Brex, a short note explaining the business purpose, and the correct expense category. For client or team meals, the note must also list the attendees.

### 3.2 Missing or lost receipts
If a receipt is lost, the employee must submit a missing-receipt declaration in Brex within 5 business days of noticing. The declaration must include the merchant, date, amount and business purpose. For amounts above 250 EUR, the declaration also needs the manager's approval in Brex. Employees with more than 3 missing-receipt declarations in 12 months will have their card use reviewed by the Finance lead.

### 3.3 Deadlines
Receipts must be uploaded within 5 business days of the transaction. In all cases, every transaction from a month must be fully documented by business day 1 after month-end, so it can be included in the close.

### 3.4 Foreign-currency receipts
Receipts in a foreign currency are uploaded as they are. Employees do not need to convert the amount; Brex and NetSuite handle the conversion.

## 4. Spending limits and approvals

### 4.1 Approval thresholds
- Up to 500 EUR: no pre-approval needed, as long as the expense follows this policy.
- From 500 to 2,500 EUR: the manager must approve before the purchase.
- Above 2,500 EUR: both the manager and the Finance lead must approve before the purchase.

### 4.2 Client meals and team meals
Client meals are allowed up to 80 EUR per person, including drinks. Team meals are allowed up to 40 EUR per person. Team events with a total cost above 500 EUR need the manager's approval before booking.

### 4.3 Software and subscriptions
New software or subscriptions of any amount must be requested through the procurement request form and approved by IT and Finance before purchase. Free trials that turn into paid plans count as new subscriptions.

### 4.4 Gifts
Client gifts are allowed up to 50 EUR per person per year. Cash, gift cards and vouchers are never allowed as gifts.

## 5. Travel

### 5.1 Booking travel
Flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel booking tool, so they are paid and recorded correctly.

### 5.2 Flights and hotels
Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class. Hotels are allowed up to 180 EUR per night, or up to 250 EUR per night in capital cities.

### 5.3 Local transport
Public transport is preferred. Taxis and ride-hailing are allowed when public transport is not practical, for example late at night or when carrying equipment.

## 6. Vendor invoices (Accounts Payable)

### 6.1 Submitting vendor invoices
Vendors must send invoices to the Accounts Payable inbox. Employees who receive an invoice directly must forward it to the Accounts Payable inbox the same day.

### 6.2 Approving vendor bills
Each vendor bill is approved in NetSuite by the budget owner. Bills must be approved within 3 business days of being received.

### 6.3 Payment terms and urgent payments
Standard payment terms are 30 days from the invoice date. Urgent payment requests must be sent to the #finance-help channel at least 2 business days before the payment is needed.

## 7. Month-end close

### 7.1 Close calendar
The books for each entity are closed by business day 6 after month-end. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays in the entity's country.

### 7.2 What employees need to do
- By business day 1: all card transactions from the month have a receipt, note and category in Brex.
- By business day 2: budget owners have approved all vendor bills from the month in NetSuite.
- By business day 3: budget owners have answered Finance's accrual requests.

### 7.3 Accruals
Budget owners must tell Finance about any service or goods received during the month but not yet invoiced, when the expected amount is above 1,000 EUR. Finance uses this to book an accrual, so the cost is recorded in the right month.

### 7.4 Reminders and escalation
Automated reminders start 3 business days before month-end. If an item is still open 2 business days after a reminder, it is escalated to the Finance lead.

## 8. Glossary

### 8.1 Accrual
An expense that has been incurred but not yet invoiced or paid. Finance estimates and records it so the cost appears in the correct month.

### 8.2 Reconciliation
Checking that the balance of an account in the books matches supporting evidence, such as a bank statement or a list of open items.

### 8.3 Month-end close
The process of making sure all transactions of the month are recorded and correct, so the financial statements of each entity can be produced and the month can be locked.

### 8.4 Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable
Accounts Payable (AP) is money the company owes to vendors. Accounts Receivable (AR) is money customers owe to the company.

### 8.5 Entity
A separate legal company within the group, usually one per country, with its own books.
